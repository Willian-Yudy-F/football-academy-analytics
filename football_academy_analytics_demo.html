The Problem

Most football academies are
flying blind on their own data.

How many students didn't renew last term?
▾
Records can sit in separate spreadsheets, making it difficult to compare participation by program or term.
→ Concept: an aggregate dashboard with clearly defined metrics.
Which attendance records need a human follow-up?
▾
A pattern can be missed when attendance is recorded inconsistently or reviewed only at the end of a term.
→ Concept: a review queue, not an automated judgement about a child.
Which student has the highest potential?
▾
Coach notes may be difficult to compare across sessions and can contain subjective assessments.
→ Concept: a reviewed progress summary with context and correction rights.
Which enrolment sources should be investigated?
▾
Source information is only useful when definitions are consistent and collection is consented.
→ Concept: aggregate source reporting after data-quality checks.

Reviewed Progress Summary · Concept

Progress reporting with
context and safeguards.

A future prototype could prepare a draft summary for an authorised coach to review before a guardian receives it. No report or email workflow is implemented here.

📊Clearly defined assessment fields
▾
Each field would need a written rubric, appropriate collection frequency and a way to distinguish observation from fact.
📈Change over time with explanation
▾
A score change should include sample size, missing sessions and a coach's explanation rather than implying certainty from a single number.
🔒Human approval before sharing
▾
A generated draft should remain private until an authorised person checks accuracy, tone, recipients and safeguarding concerns.
